# Document Management

## Current status

**Planned.** Documents can be generated or exported in several workflows, but Odyssey does not currently document a general-purpose document repository.

## Existing boundaries

- PDF generation is documented in [`pdf-generation.md`](pdf-generation.md).
- Tax exports are versioned and hashed in [`tax-compliance.md`](tax-compliance.md).
- The roadmap identifies transaction attachments and e-signatures as remaining work.
- RBAC protects access to the records and exports that already exist.

## Deferred scope

Central document storage, contracts/SOP/certificate libraries, employee-document management, versioning, approval queues, digital signatures, retention policies, full-text search, and document-level permissions.
